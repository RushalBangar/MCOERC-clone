Dr. Devidas D. Dighe
Controller of Examination

As the Controller of Examinations at Matoshri College of Engineering & Research Centre Nashik, I am thrilled to welcome you. My role is to oversee and manage the entire examination process, ensuring fairness and transparency for all students.

Examinations are a vital part of your academic journey, serving as a measure of your knowledge, skills, and understanding of your subjects. At MCERC, we are committed to maintaining high academic standards and a strong examination framework to ensure integrity.

We are dedicated to providing a well-organized and supportive environment for your exams. Our team works diligently to ensure that exams run smoothly, following established schedules and protocols, and creating a calm atmosphere where you can perform at your best.

As you prepare, use effective study techniques, manage your time wisely, and seek help from your instructors as needed. Stay focused and confident—your efforts will be reflected in your results.

Approach your exams with a positive mindset, viewing them as an opportunity for growth and self-assessment. They are more than just a means to a grade; they are a chance to reinforce your knowledge and develop essential skills for your future.

The Examination Department is here to support you throughout this process. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us. We are here to assist you.

I wish you the very best in your upcoming exams. May you achieve the success you aim for and demonstrate your full potential.

Best wishes to all for a bright future!!!
