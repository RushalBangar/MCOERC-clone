Equal Opportunity Center

“Equal Opportunity Center” is established in the college from academic year 2022-23. This centre has been established for the purpose of guidance of scholarship / freeship scheme, competitive examination to the backward class students admitted in the college in the academic year 2022-23, as well as entrepreneurship, business, employment generation - guidance communication campaign for economic justice.

Also, to start the youth dialogue program, ‘Equal Opportunity Center' has been established in the college, for which the following teaching, non-teaching staff and students have been included.
