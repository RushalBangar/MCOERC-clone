Welcome to MCOERC

A Legacy of
Technical Excellence

Established in 2008 at Eklahare, Nashik, Matoshri College of Engineering & Research Centre is committed to providing world-class technical education. With NBA & NAAC accreditation and autonomous status, we offer 4-year B.Tech, M.Tech, and MCA programmes designed to meet the rigorous demands of the global industry.
