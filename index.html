Empowering through Quality Technical Education

Established in 2008 at Eklahare, Nashik, Matoshri College of Engineering & Research Centre is committed to providing world-class technical education. We offer cutting-edge 4-year B.Tech, M.Tech, and MCA programmes designed to meet global industry standards.
