Welcome to MCOERC

A Legacy of
Technical Excellence

Established in 2008 at Eklahare, Nashik, Matoshri College of Engineering & Research Centre is committed to providing world-class technical education. With NBA & NAAC accreditation, we offer cutting-edge 4-year B.Tech, M.Tech, and MCA programmes.

"To Establish an Omnipotent Learning Centre Meeting the Standards to Evolve as a Lighthouse for the Society."

  • Setting up state-of-the-art infrastructure
  • Empowering through quality technical education
  • Developing as a prominent Research centre
