Eligibility Criteria
For the admission to the Ph.D programme in a related subject in the faculty, applicants fulfilling the following criteria shall be treated as eligible:
  1. Person having a post Graduate Degree(Master Degree) Examination with at least 50% marks or equivalent Grade Point Average(GPA).
  2. 5% relaxation for reserve category students of Maharashtra State only.
  3. Candidate must have qualified PET conducted by SPPU.
Documents Required
Documents required for admission for open and reserved category students:
  1. Attested copy of previous result of Ph. D. Entrance Examination Paper- I (if applicable)
  2. Final year M.E/M.tech Mark sheet and certificates
  3. Final year degree Mark sheet and certificates
  4. Statement of Marks – SSC (10th)
  5. Statement of Marks – HSC (12th) (if applicable)
  6. Final year diploma mark sheet (if applicable)
  7. Leaving Certificate / Transfer Certificate
  8. Migration Certificate (if applicable)
  9. Certificate of Indian Nationality in the name of the Candidate
  10. Domicile Certificate (if applicable), clearly indicating the place of Permanent Residence of the Candidate / Father or Mother or Husband
  11. Aadhaar Card
