Innovation and Incubation Centre

The main objective to establish the Innovation and Incubation Center in campus is to provide the experimental set up, modern tools, test beds and learning resources for the researchers to carry out experimentations, which is aimed at bridging the research gap between academics and industry. The objectives are,

  • To motivate the faculty and students for research and innovation.
  • To develop the research culture and research attitude in the campus.
  • To procure and deploy the modern equipments and tools.
  • To orient the researchers for exploring opportunities related to industry consultancy
  • To help the research scholars to implement and test their research ideas.
