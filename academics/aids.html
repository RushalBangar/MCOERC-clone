Artificial Intelligence and Data Science is an evolving branch of Computer Engineering that deals with processing and retrieval of knowledge from structured and unstructured data using scientific methodologies, processes, and techniques drawn from various domains such as Statistics, Cognitive Science, Computing and Information Science. This knowledge is used in commercial applications to make various intelligent decisions. Data science and Artificial Intelligence are both concerned with gathering, categorising, planning, analysing, and interpreting data. Together they deal with the creation of data-driven solutions, data visualisation tools, and methodologies for analysing large amounts of data, for solving numerous computational and real-world problems.

Artificial Intelligence and Data Science are the current industry trends, and they promise niche and well-paid career. Artificial intelligence and data science largely contribute to create a variety of clever business solutions. The research in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science has a significant impact on the manufacturing, e-commerce, banking, finance, transportation, and healthcare industries.

During this programme, you will learn how to design, create, and implement software solutions using AI and DS to help businesses solve real-world problems. In this programme you'll learn about topics like Artificial Intelligence, Data Analytics, Data Visualization, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Semantic Web and Social Network Analysis, Block Chain technologies, and Data Security & Privacy.

AI and DS graduates are capable of designing, developing, and using open source technologies and tools to suggest solutions for industrial problems. They can evaluate how engineering knowledge and practices meet ethical, legitimate, proficient, and social standards. They can also demonstrate knowledge of data handling, knowledge extraction, mobile and distributed application development, intelligent web/e-commerce development, database administration, computer hardware, networking, education & training and decision support systems using Artificial Intelligence and Data Science tools and techniques.
