Dr. Varsha H. Patil
Dean Academics

As the Dean of Academics at Matoshri College of Engineering and Research Centre, Eklahare, Nashik, I am delighted to be a part of a forward-thinking institution that prioritizes excellence in education and personal growth. Our college stands as a beacon of innovation, driven by our commitment to core values such as positivity, respect, integrity, wisdom, and empathy.

At MCERC, we believe in offering a curriculum that is both rigorous and relevant, designed to equip our students with the skills and knowledge required to excel in their chosen fields. Our autonomous status empowers us to continually refine and enhance our academic offerings, ensuring they meet the evolving needs of society and industry.

As an autonomous institute, we emphasize both values and skills through our academic programs, offering a rigorous and relevant curriculum. Our student-centric pedagogy, supported by skilled and motivated professionals, provides diverse opportunities for students to enhance their learning beyond traditional methods. With inquiry at the heart of our teaching approaches, we empower students to learn both independently and collaboratively. Supported by a team of dedicated and skilled professionals, we provide opportunities that extend beyond traditional learning, encouraging students to explore and innovate.

We understand the importance of a respectful and inclusive atmosphere in achieving outstanding learning outcomes. It is within this nurturing environment that our students develop the teamwork and leadership skills necessary to succeed in the 21st century.

As we look to the future, our vision is clear: to cultivate lifelong learners who take charge of their education and make meaningful contributions to their communities.

Wishing everyone a successful and fulfilling journey ahead!

Warm regards,

MCOERC
